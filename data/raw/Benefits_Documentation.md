# Employee Benefits Documentation

**Document Owner:** Human Resources Department
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025
**Applies To:** All full-time employees of NimbusTech Solutions Pvt. Ltd.

## 1. Health Insurance

- NimbusTech provides **Group Mediclaim Insurance** covering the employee,
  spouse, and up to 2 dependent children, with a base sum insured of
  **INR 5,00,000** per family per year.
- Employees may opt for a **top-up plan** (additional INR 5,00,000) at a
  subsidized premium deducted from monthly payroll.
- Parents/parents-in-law can be added under a voluntary parental cover at
  the employee's cost, with a sum insured of **INR 3,00,000**.
- Cashless treatment is available at over 4,000 network hospitals across India.

## 2. Life and Accident Insurance

- All employees are covered under a **Group Term Life Insurance** policy
  equal to **3x annual CTC**, at no cost to the employee.
- Group Personal Accident Insurance covers **5x annual CTC** in case of
  accidental death or permanent disability.

## 3. Retirement Benefits

- **Employee Provident Fund (EPF):** 12% of basic salary contributed by both
  employee and employer, as per statutory norms.
- **Gratuity:** Payable after 5 years of continuous service, as per the
  Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972.
- **National Pension Scheme (NPS):** Optional employer-matched contribution
  up to 10% of basic salary.

## 4. Wellness Benefits

- Annual health check-up for employees above 30, and for all employees above
  the Manager level.
- Subsidized gym membership reimbursement up to **INR 2,000 per month**.
- Free access to an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) offering confidential
  counseling for mental health, financial planning, and legal advice.

## 5. Family and Parental Benefits

- Maternity Leave: 26 weeks paid leave (see Leave Policy for details).
- Paternity Leave: 10 working days paid leave.
- Crèche reimbursement of up to **INR 5,000 per month** for children below 6 years.
- Adoption leave of 12 weeks for primary caregivers.

## 6. Learning and Development

- Annual learning allowance of **INR 25,000** per employee for certifications,
  courses, or conference attendance, subject to manager approval.
- Access to an internal learning platform with curated technical and
  leadership courses.
- Tuition reimbursement of up to **INR 1,50,000** for job-relevant higher
  education, subject to a 2-year service commitment post-completion.

## 7. Other Perks

- Meal vouchers/subsidized cafeteria at all office locations.
- Employee referral bonus of **INR 25,000–75,000** depending on the role level.
- Annual team outing and festival celebration budget per department.
- Flexible Benefit Plan (FBP) allowing employees to structure a portion of
  CTC across meal cards, LTA, and telephone reimbursement for tax efficiency.

## 8. Claim Process

- Insurance claims (cashless or reimbursement) are processed through the
  designated Third-Party Administrator (TPA) portal.
- Wellness and learning reimbursements are claimed via the HR portal with
  supporting invoices, and are typically processed within **10 working days**.

## 9. Contact

For benefits-related queries, contact **benefits@nimbustech.example.com**.
